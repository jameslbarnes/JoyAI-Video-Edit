Potential next performance improvement, not implemented: the currently installed FluxRT IFNet accepts only midpoint interpolation and uses recursive passes for 4x, constraining practical fixed output rates to 2x/4x native rates. Upstream [RIFE supports arbitrary timestep interpolation](https://github.com/hzwer/ECCV2022-RIFE). A newer compatible model plus a timestamp-based sampler could test 8 or 10 native fps to exactly 24 output fps, allocating more work to the model GPU and less to interpolation. This is an inference about a future integration, not a measured result or a capability of the current wrapper. No new model weights were downloaded.
