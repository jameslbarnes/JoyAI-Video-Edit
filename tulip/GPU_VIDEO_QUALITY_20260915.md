# Mobile compression quality trial

September 16 guarded retry after the user approved 20 Mbps with both CUDA priority fixes active: `logs/retest-gpu-bitrate-prioritized-20260915.py` changed bitrate only, then automatically restored 15 Mbps when a 282.36 ms delivery gap was recorded with 23 frames queued. The event occurred 5.25 seconds into monitoring after the new publisher had already sent more than 64 frames; the brief trial did not establish perceptual quality or bitrate causation. Evidence: sibling SDK `logs/restart-20260916-000027/bitrate-audit.jsonl`.

The read-only rollback check then reproduced a 306.44 ms delivery gap at 15 Mbps with 33 frames queued: GPU prepare took 268.88 ms, NVENC 0.77 ms, post-sync 7.36 ms and pipe writing 3.12 ms. Over 61.32 seconds fresh output averaged 31.865 fps with zero queue drops. Thus the remaining intermittent stall also exists at the baseline bitrate and differs from the earlier empty-queue events. This is GPU preparation waiting, not established network bandwidth causation; the underlying reason for that wait remains unisolated. Recovery evidence: sibling SDK `logs/restart-20260916-000128/recovery-summary.json`; local receiver evidence: sibling Etherea `logs/gpu-20m-retry-rollback-whep-20260916.jsonl`, which also recorded a freeze without packet loss. Mobile remained connected to joyai-gpu. Current bitrate is 15 Mbps, with 1440p and both priority fixes retained. Do not describe this recovery interval as stall-free or claim 20 Mbps is proven to cause jitter.

Update after user feedback: the user still observed jitter at 20 Mbps. Restored 15 Mbps via SDK trial `logs/restart-20260915-232612/`: sender 32.000 fps and zero drops in the first 90 seconds. A subsequent two-minute local receiver (`gpu-jitter-15m-whep-20260915.jsonl`) nevertheless recorded one 297 ms freeze, without dropped frames or packet loss. Therefore higher bitrate is not established as the cause, and the earlier short 20 Mbps acceptance result did not establish smoothness on the phone. Further GPU scheduling investigation follows in the jitter diagnostics; 20 Mbps is no longer live.

The user's request was to improve quality after the GPU transport deployment. The 2592 x 1440 stream retained the old 1080p bitrate of 15 Mbps. Tested bitrate alone, preserving native generation, RIFE 2x, the SR model and denoise value, encoder P4/ultra-low-latency, zero B frames and zero lookahead. These trials change delivered compression quality, not the model's native detail.

24 Mbps trial: SDK `logs/restart-20260915-231947/bitrate-summary.json` measured 31.886 fresh fps over about 90 seconds with zero sender drops. Etherea `logs/gpu-24m-whep-20260915.jsonl` recorded one 322 ms freeze about 36 seconds after receiver startup, with no packet loss. The sender also advanced fewer frames over that interval. This was not the initial reconnect, but causation by bitrate is unproven; live Cue/content and workstation scheduling were not controlled. Do not report this trial as freeze-free. Retested a more conservative 20 Mbps setting.

20 Mbps initially passed the short trial. SDK `logs/restart-20260915-232228/bitrate-summary.json`: 31.997 fresh fps, zero sender drops, maximum queue 29/48 over about 90 seconds. Etherea `logs/gpu-20m-whep-20260915.jsonl`: 2063 frames decoded at 2592 x 1440, zero dropped frames, freezes or packet loss in the 65-second probe. The diagnostic receiver exited normally. This was a short acceptance test, not a long-term stability result; subsequent user feedback led to rollback as described above.

Each config edit was atomic and backed up; SDK-side trial script auto-restores 15 Mbps on sender failure. Config recreation briefly restarts only the GPU encoder/remux publisher. The same mobile peer reconnected to joyai-gpu after each change. Local receiver statistics do not establish phone-side decoding smoothness, and no matched-image quality metric was collected. The user was asked to assess texture clarity and motion in the app.

NVIDIA reference consulted for encoder controls and quality/latency tradeoffs: https://docs.nvidia.com/video-technologies/video-codec-sdk/13.0/nvenc-video-encoder-api-prog-guide/ . Higher presets and adaptive quantization are future candidates requiring separate validation, not changes in this trial.

To revert this trial, change only gpu_video.bitrate back to 15000000 in the local output-presentation.json, using an atomic replacement. Preserve all other fields and expect a short receiver reconnect. Do not restore old complete stack configs.
